The Human Digestive System

Hi Riyansh! 👋

Here's an amazing fact to start your day: your digestive system is about 9 metres long — that's roughly the length of a school bus! All of that is coiled up inside your body, working hard every single time you eat. Pretty incredible, right?

This week, we're going on a journey through your body to discover what happens to food from the moment you take your first bite to... well, the very end! 😄

📺 This Week's Video

Digestive System | The Dr. Binocs Show

🤔 Before You Watch — Make a Prediction!

Before pressing play, think about this and write down your answer:

"How many different organs do you think your food passes through from your mouth to the end? Take a guess — we'll see if you're right!"

👀 While You Watch — Look Out For These!

Try to answer these three questions as you watch the video:

  1. What is the job of saliva (spit) in your mouth? — Why is chewing so important before you swallow?
  2. What does the stomach use to break down food? — Hint: it's something quite strong and acidic!
  3. What happens in the small intestine? — This is where the real magic happens. What does your body take from the food here?

✏️ After You Watch — Your Mission!

Now that you've watched the video, here's your activity:

Draw a simple diagram of the digestive system. Label at least 5 organs (mouth, oesophagus, stomach, small intestine, large intestine). Next to each organ, write one sentence explaining what it does. You can use colours to make it look cool!

Once you're done, you can ask Dad to take a photo of your drawing or type up your answers.

💡 Did You Know?

Your stomach acid is so strong that it could dissolve metal! But don't worry — your stomach has a special lining of mucus that protects it and replaces itself every few days. Your body is basically a superhero! 🦸
