Question 1: The Number Machine

A machine takes a number, multiplies it by 3, then subtracts 5.

If the machine outputs 19, what number was put in?

Step-by-step explanation:

The machine does two things: multiply by 3, then subtract 5.

To find the input, we work backwards:
1. The output is 19
2. Undo "subtract 5" by adding 5: 19 + 5 = 24
3. Undo "multiply by 3" by dividing by 3: 24 ÷ 3 = 8

Check: 8 × 3 = 24, then 24 − 5 = 19 ✔

Correct answer: 8

Question 2: The Coin Jar

Riyansh has a jar with coins. He counts them in groups of 4 and has 3 left over. He counts them in groups of 5 and has 2 left over.

If the jar has fewer than 30 coins, how many coins are in the jar?

(Hint: Try numbers that leave remainder 3 when divided by 4, and check which also leave remainder 2 when divided by 5.)

Step-by-step explanation:

We need a number less than 30 that:
• Leaves remainder 3 when divided by 4
• Leaves remainder 2 when divided by 5

Numbers with remainder 3 when ÷ 4: 3, 7, 11, 15, 19, 23, 27

Now check which of these gives remainder 2 when ÷ 5:
• 3 ÷ 5 = 0 remainder 3 ✘
• 7 ÷ 5 = 1 remainder 2 ✔ Yes!
• 27 ÷ 5 = 5 remainder 2 ✔ Also works!

Both 7 and 27 are valid answers!

Correct answers: 7 or 27

Question 3: The Garden Path

A rectangular garden is 12 m long and 5 m wide. A path of width 1 m runs around the outside of the garden.

What is the area of the path (in m²)?

(Hint: Think about the bigger rectangle that includes the path.)

Step-by-step explanation:

The path is 1 m wide and goes around the outside, so the bigger rectangle is:

• Length: 12 + 1 + 1 = 14 m (1 m extra on each side)
• Width: 5 + 1 + 1 = 7 m (1 m extra on each side)

Area of big rectangle: 14 × 7 = 98 m²
Area of garden: 12 × 5 = 60 m²

Area of path = Big rectangle − Garden
= 98 − 60 = 38 m²

Correct answer: 38

Question 4: The Handshake Problem

There are 6 people at a party. If every person shakes hands with every other person exactly once, how many handshakes happen in total?

Step-by-step explanation:

Let's think about it person by person:

• Person 1 shakes hands with 5 others
• Person 2 shakes hands with 4 new people (already shook with Person 1)
• Person 3 shakes hands with 3 new people
• Person 4 shakes hands with 2 new people
• Person 5 shakes hands with 1 new person
• Person 6 has already shaken everyone's hand!

Total = 5 + 4 + 3 + 2 + 1 = 15 handshakes

Formula: n(n−1) ÷ 2 = 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15

Correct answer: 15

Question 5: The Treasure Chest Code

A treasure chest has a 3-digit code lock. You know these clues:

• All three digits add up to 12
• The hundreds digit is double the ones digit
• The tens digit is 4 more than the ones digit

What is the 3-digit code?

(Hint: Let the ones digit = some number, then figure out the other two digits using the clues.)

Step-by-step explanation:

Let's call the ones digit x.

From the clues:
• Hundreds digit = 2x (double the ones digit)
• Tens digit = x + 4 (4 more than the ones digit)

All three add up to 12:
2x + (x + 4) + x = 12
4x + 4 = 12
4x = 8
x = 2

So the digits are:
• Ones digit = 2
• Hundreds digit = 2 × 2 = 4
• Tens digit = 2 + 4 = 6

The code is: 462

Check: 4 + 6 + 2 = 12 ✔ | Hundreds (4) = double ones (2) ✔ | Tens (6) = ones (2) + 4 ✔

Correct answer: 462
